The desolate borderlands of Dvor Ineya. The provincial captiol of [[Krepost Veles]] is one of the few truly populated regions in the entire oblast. The environment is brutal, requiring any who may find themselves within Nav Krai's borders to endure extreme conditions. However, the environs are not the only cause for its lack of population. Souls that dwell in Nav Krai are particularly prone to corruption, making the area nigh impossible for civilians to inhabit. To combat this, the headquarters of the [[Lightkeepers]] was established in this region. The majority of the [[Lightkeepers]]' forces are stationed here to fend off the impending threat of corruption, though there are smaller "Lighthouses" in each Oblast which serve as the base of operations for the [[Lightkeepers]] in the region. 

Nav Krai is physically isolated from the rest of the nation, enclosed by the [[Smorodina Mountains]]
