While the current Lunnaya Tsaritsa's reign began shortly after the enlightenment, the title itself can refer to any of its previous holders throughout history. The Lunnaya Tsaritsa's role is not only that of governance, acting as an envoy of the [[Lady of Dormition]] in the material plane. Considered the highest authority in the land, she presides over the three governing bodies, resolving any disputes among them.
