Duty bound to serve the inhabitants of Dvor Ineya, the Lightkeepers use a combination of clerical and martial prowess to shepherd lost souls and subdue those who have endured corruption. Unlike in many other cultures, the Lightkeepers' role as psychopomps is revered rather than feared. Believed by the general populace to be servants of the [[Lady of Dormition]] herself, they are a rather enigmatic organization. Only those within their ranks or of considerable status in Dvor Ineya's government have a true grasp of their duties.
