A pseudo-subspecies of fey native to Dvor Ineya, though they are more akin to an ethnic group than a truly distinct variety of fey. The details behind the appearance of fey in Dvor Ineya are shrouded in mystery. The people of Dvor Ineya believe that the [[Lady of Dormition]] had personally sculpted them using her mystical ice to serve as her retainers during her slumber. Many historians from other regions, however, instead believe that the fey of Dvor Ineya originated from another plane entirely. The veracity of either of these claims is uncertain. However, much to the confusion of scholars around the world, compelling evidence for each story of origin has surfaced.

The Frostfaeries are a hardy people, valuing more concrete and practical skills and attributes. Their pursuance of the arts has served more as a distraction from their harsh conditions than as a true exploration of one's spiritual and intellectual self.
