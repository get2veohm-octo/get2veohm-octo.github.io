Dvor Ineya--also known as the Court of Frost--is the homeland of most [[Unseelie Fey]] in the Material Plane. Governed by the [[Lunnaya Tsaritsa]], it is a frigid and mountainous island nation located in the northern reaches of Alteres.
### Geography/Terrain
While the outer reaches of the nation are more level, the inner regions are highly mountainous, requiring specialized infrastructure to settle.

### Government
Headed by the [[Lunnaya Tsaritsa]] herself, the governing system is divided into 3 branches. [[The Ecclesiarchy]] representing religious interests, [[The Ledyanoy Veche]] representing the peoples voices in legislative matters, and [[The High Court of Morana]] representing the primary judicial authority. The leaders of these branches often have conflicts due to differing interests of the parties they represent, but the Tsaritsa is able to bring balance and resolve these disputes. In addition, each Oblast is represented by a [[Vestniki Zimy|Vestnik Zimy]]. The [[Vestniki Zimy]] act as instruments of the Tsaritsa's will throughout the land, rooting out treason and corruption.
### Religion
The only deity actively worshipped in Dvor Ineya is the [[Lady of Dormition]],
represented by the [[Church of Repose]] and the [[Lunnaya Tsaritsa]] herself.
